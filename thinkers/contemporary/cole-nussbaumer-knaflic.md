# Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic (active 2010s--present)
> "The chart is not the destination -- the story is."

## Overview

Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic is the author of the most widely read beginner guide to data visualization for business, the founder of the "storytelling with data" methodology and community, and a former data visualization educator at Google. Active from the 2010s to the present, Knaflic has had an outsized impact on how business professionals think about and practice data communication.

Her *Storytelling with Data* is the single most commonly assigned data visualization book in corporate training and business school curricula, having sold over a million copies. Knaflic's contribution is to translate the principles of effective visualization -- drawn from [Tufte](../modern/edward-tufte.md), [Cleveland](../modern/william-cleveland.md), and the perceptual science tradition -- into an accessible, step-by-step methodology that non-specialists can immediately apply.

## Key Contributions

- Developed the **six-step "storytelling with data" methodology**: understand context, choose appropriate visual, eliminate clutter, focus attention, think like a designer, tell a story
- Authored the most widely read data visualization book in business contexts
- Created a global community and training ecosystem around the "storytelling with data" brand
- Translated academic visualization principles into accessible practice for non-specialists
- Former **data visualization educator at Google**, where she developed internal training programs
- Demonstrated that visualization skills can be taught systematically to business audiences

## Major Works

## Core Philosophy

Knaflic's six-step process provides a clear, repeatable methodology for creating effective data visualizations:

1. **Understand context** -- Who is your audience? What do they need to know?
2. **Choose an appropriate visual** -- Select the chart type that best serves the message
3. **Eliminate clutter** -- Remove everything that does not directly serve comprehension
4. **Focus attention** -- Use pre-attentive attributes (color, size, position) to guide the viewer's eye
5. **Think like a designer** -- Apply design principles of affordance, accessibility, and aesthetics
6. **Tell a story** -- Structure the visualization within a narrative arc with a clear beginning, middle, and end

She emphasizes that visualization is always in service of a specific audience, making a specific point. The chart is not the destination -- the story is. This narrative-first approach distinguishes her work from more tool-centric or data-centric approaches.

## Connections

- Acknowledges the influence of [Edward Tufte](../modern/edward-tufte.md) (eliminate clutter, maximize data-ink ratio) and [William Cleveland](../modern/william-cleveland.md) (choose appropriate encodings based on perceptual effectiveness)
- Translates these academic principles into accessible practice for non-specialists
- Her work sits firmly in the [narrative/storytelling school](../../schools/narrative-storytelling.md) of visualization
- Shares [Stephanie Evergreen's](./stephanie-evergreen.md) commitment to practical, audience-first visualization, though Knaflic focuses on business contexts while Evergreen targets research and evaluation
- Complements [Alberto Cairo's](./alberto-cairo.md) ethical framework with a practitioner-oriented methodology
- Part of the [minimalist/Tuftean tradition](../../schools/minimalist-tuftean.md) in her emphasis on eliminating clutter and focusing attention

## Influence

*Storytelling with Data* is the single most commonly assigned data visualization book in corporate training and business school curricula. It has sold over a million copies and been translated into dozens of languages, making it one of the most commercially successful data visualization books ever published.

Knaflic's impact is primarily in the corporate and business world, where she has helped millions of professionals move from default Excel charts to intentional, audience-focused data communication. Her six-step methodology provides a concrete, teachable framework that organizations can adopt at scale.

The "storytelling with data" brand has expanded into a community ecosystem including workshops, a podcast, a blog, and practice exercises, creating a self-sustaining movement of practitioners who share before-and-after transformations of their visualizations. This community-building aspect of her work has amplified her influence far beyond the books themselves.
