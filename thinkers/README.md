# The DataViz Pantheon

37 thinkers across three eras who shaped the theory, practice, and culture of data visualization. Each entry is a standalone profile covering contributions, philosophy, major works, and connections to the broader field.

## Historical Pioneers (pre-1950)

Thinkers who laid the foundations — inventing chart forms, establishing the use of data for public argument, and creating the theoretical vocabulary of visual information.

| Thinker | Years | Key Contribution |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| [John Graunt](historical/john-graunt.md) | 1620–1674 | Founder of demography; inventor of the life table |
| [William Playfair](historical/william-playfair.md) | 1759–1823 | Inventor of the line chart, bar chart, and pie chart |
| [Florence Nightingale](historical/florence-nightingale.md) | 1820–1910 | Pioneer of data visualization for policy advocacy |
| [Charles Joseph Minard](historical/charles-joseph-minard.md) | 1781–1870 | Creator of "the best statistical graphic ever drawn" |
| [John Snow](historical/john-snow.md) | 1813–1858 | Founder of spatial epidemiology via the cholera map |
| [W.E.B. Du Bois](historical/web-du-bois.md) | 1868–1963 | Pioneer of data visualization for social justice |
| [Otto Neurath](historical/otto-neurath.md) | 1882–1945 | Creator of ISOTYPE pictogram system |
| [Willard Brinton](historical/willard-brinton.md) | 1880–1957 | Author of the first business graphics textbook |
| [Jacques Bertin](historical/jacques-bertin.md) | 1918–2010 | Founder of visual semiotics; the seven visual variables |

## Modern Founders (1950–2000)

The generation that built data visualization into a recognized field — providing scientific foundations, defining aesthetic standards, and creating the grammars and frameworks still in use today.

| Thinker | Years | Key Contribution |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| [Edward Tufte](modern/edward-tufte.md) | 1942–present | The field's most influential theorist and popularizer |
| [John Tukey](modern/john-tukey.md) | 1915–2000 | Father of exploratory data analysis; inventor of the box plot |
| [Howard Wainer](modern/howard-wainer.md) | 1943–present | Champion of graphical literacy; visualization historian |
| [William Cleveland](modern/william-cleveland.md) | 1943–present | Pioneer of perceptual science for chart design |
| [Jock Mackinlay](modern/jock-mackinlay.md) | ~1953–present | Creator of APT; automated visualization design |
| [Ben Shneiderman](modern/ben-shneiderman.md) | 1947–present | Inventor of the treemap; interactive visualization pioneer |
| [Stuart Card](modern/stuart-card.md) | 1943–present | Co-founder of information visualization as a discipline |
| [Leland Wilkinson](modern/leland-wilkinson.md) | 1944–2023 | Creator of the Grammar of Graphics |
| [Michael Friendly](modern/michael-friendly.md) | ~1944–present | Historian of data visualization; HistData maintainer |
